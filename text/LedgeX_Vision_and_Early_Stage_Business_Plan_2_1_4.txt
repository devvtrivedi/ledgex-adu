02 / COMPANY VISION

The intelligence layer for distributed residential development
LedgeX begins with a high-utility parcel-level underwriting platform designed to help a professional evaluate whether an ADU or
redevelopment concept may work financially. The longer-term thesis is conditional: if paid use, source controls and outcome
validation support it, LedgeX could aggregate fragmented property, feasibility, project-intent and outcome data into a
standardised market layer. The early product may succeed as a focused vertical software product. Any venture-scale path would
require repeated use, validated local exceptions and real user intent before supporting standardised project inventory, enterprise
intelligence, transaction routing or future financing infrastructure.

   CORE SEQUENCE
   Credible automated Property File → paid professional use observed → repeat analysis → structured exceptions → validated
   outcomes → possible standardised inventory → potential later lender or transaction infrastructure.

The company is deliberately not claiming that the full sequence exists today. Each step is an evidence question. The next
commercial milestone is payment and repeat use for the automated Property File, supported by measured exception frequency,
accuracy, refusal rate and cost.

Strategic principles
• Utility before scale: the first output must help a professional make or avoid a real decision.
• Evidence before feature breadth: add capabilities when they reduce a named uncertainty.
• Provenance before polish: every fact has a date, source and exact endpoint or document.
• Refusal before categorical conclusions: conflicts and gaps are named, never silently resolved and never quietly filled.
• Automation before headcount: a file that requires a person to finish is not a product; it is a service with a software wrapper.
  LedgeX is building the former.
• Professional payment before consumer volume: a small number of paid workflows is more informative than unpriced
  attention.

03 / THE PROBLEM

The information exists; the organised market does not
Millions of residential parcels contain potential additional housing capacity, but the market cannot price that capacity efficiently.
Relevant information is fragmented across county parcel records, city zoning and permit systems, state ADU rules, hazard and
environmental maps, property-tax records, contractor estimates, rent and sales data, lender standards and owner assumptions.

A professional evaluating a property must assemble the file manually, determine which source controls, reconcile what a record
says against what geometry and permit history suggest, and translate the result into a financial decision. The work is slow,
inconsistent and difficult to reconstruct later.

The result
• Builders and designers spend scarce pre-bid time on poorly qualified properties.
• Agents and investors cannot consistently price development optionality.
• Homeowners cannot distinguish a useful screening signal from a determination that requires professional review.
• Lenders lack standardised project inventory and a visible chain of assumptions.
• Potentially viable housing projects remain unpriced, delayed or abandoned.

04 / THE AUTOMATED OPERATING MODEL

Fully automated production and delivery
   THE CONTROLLING OPERATING STATEMENT
   There is zero per-file human review before delivery. A Property File is composed and delivered by machine within a single
   request. No person reads, checks, completes, approves or releases an individual customer file. There is no review queue, no
   escalation path, no pending state and no deferred order. This replaces the Concierge delivery model described in Business Plan 2.1
   §05, which is superseded and must not be reintroduced without a new controlling revision.

This is the single largest change from Business Plan 2.1, and it changes staffing, operations, pricing, turnaround and unit

workflow — it is a different product with a different boundary.

06 / CONFIDENCE SYSTEM

Deterministic High, Medium and Low
Confidence is a disclosure mechanism. It tells a professional how much weight a field or conclusion can bear. It is calculated by
documented, deterministic rules — not by a model, not by judgement, and not by a score whose derivation cannot be reproduced.

   THE DISTINCTION THAT MATTERS MOST
   High, Medium and Low do not mean “good, uncertain and missing.” All three levels describe evidence that exists and was
   retrieved. Data that is missing, inaccessible, legally restricted or unavailable is a different category entirely and is never expressed
   as Low confidence. Conflating the two would let an absent record masquerade as a weak answer, which is precisely the failure the
   product exists to prevent.

How missing evidence is handled
Where evidence is missing, inaccessible, legally restricted or unavailable, the system applies the following response, in order:

1. Omit the unsupported fact. It is recorded as unavailable, with the reason, and shown as such.
2. Downgrade the dependent conclusion where the remaining evidence still permits it — using a weaker but valid source,
   lowering confidence, and naming the substitution.
3. Refuse the dependent conclusion where it cannot be supported. The refusal is stated by name with the missing input
   identified. It is never silently dropped.
4. Refuse the complete file where the missing evidence prevents the core product from being produced at all.

   THE CASCADE IS THE SAFETY ARGUMENT
   If recorded map history cannot be retrieved, SB 9 eligibility is not quietly absent from the file and it certainly is not computed as
   though no prior lot split occurred — it is refused, by name, with the missing input stated. Automated delivery is only defensible
   if every unmet input propagates visibly to every conclusion that required it. A partial file that looks complete is worse than no file.

Confidence record-keeping
• Every confidence result retains its confidence rule ID — which documented rule produced the grade — and the identifiers of
  the supporting evidence.
• Every fact retains its source, exact endpoint, retrieval date, source publication date, stated cadence and observed licence, so any
  grade can be reconstructed as of its date.

• Confidence rules are versioned. A file produced today can be reproduced later with the rule version that was in force.

   WHAT CONFIDENCE IS NOT
   The confidence system improves disclosure. It does not clear a data licence, does not replace counsel review, does not
   constitute professional advice, and is not a guarantee of accuracy. A High-confidence field drawn from a source whose
   commercial terms are unconfirmed is still blocked from sale. Confidence and rights are independent gates and are never traded off

08 / SAN JOSÉ — PHASE 1 LAUNCH JURISDICTION

12 / COMPOUNDING DATA ASSET AND PRODUCT VISION

What compounds, and what no longer does
Business Plan 2.1 identified the durable asset as a corpus of manual resolution steps accumulated through Concierge delivery.
That asset is forfeited by this revision and this section is restated honestly rather than quietly re-pointed at something else.

What still compounds
• Customer reports and outcome data may identify discrepancies, but corrected property facts enter only
   through approved machine retrieval or deterministic derivation - never by hand.
• Verified feasibility assumptions and realised project outcomes — actual permit outcomes, approved unit sizes, realised
  rents and valuations, projects pursued versus abandoned.
• Contractor cost performance observed over time, replacing purchased cost bands.
• The rule corpus itself — dated, cited, versioned local rules with their source text, which is expensive to assemble and
  improves with every jurisdiction.
• Observed source failures and schema drift by jurisdiction, logged as structured data. This directly reduces the marginal
  cost of the next jurisdiction.
• Exception truth sets built by internal analysis, which tune detectors and reduce false-positive rate over time.

information with source provenance, confidence, rights controls and explicit unavailable-information treatment. This is the minimum paid
product. It does not require a hypothetical placement to be useful.
